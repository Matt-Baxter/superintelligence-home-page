From source to story

1. Focus on relevant progress
Sources include papers, labs, research organizations, forums and newsletters. Stories must relate to progress toward advanced AI or its implications.
2. Compare coverage
Duplicate coverage is grouped. Titles, summaries and links point back to the original publishers; the reader does not host full articles.
3. Inspect the explanation
Topics show matched terms. Significance scores summarize configured signals; they are a reading aid, not a guarantee of scientific quality or truth.
4. Build your reading routine
Browse and filter the actual feed without an account. Sign in to vote and save stories for later. Your saved stories belong to your account. Administration tools require an admin account.
