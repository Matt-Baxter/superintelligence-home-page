# UI, UX and navigation review (Nov 9, 2026)

Branch `feature/ui-review-2026-11-09`, from `feature/john-combined-2026-11-10` (2b9377f). Method: the extended site with
sample data, signed in as admin, every page the navigation offers (23) captured at 1280×800 and 390×844, an axe-core
scan (WCAG 2 A/AA plus best practices), and layout facts per page (headings, buttons, tap targets under 24px, sideways
overflow at phone width, console errors, load time). The capture script is `test/_ui-capture.js` (review tooling,
not part of the suite).

Baseline that holds up: every page loads (200, about 1.1 to 1.3 s on sample data), no console errors, no sideways
scroll on phones, one h1 per page, and the accessibility scan is mostly clean.

9. **Story meta on phones** still wraps the source kind and time onto a second line (now aligned, #9). Folding the

## Monochrome pass, Nov 10

Branch `feature/ui-monochrome-2026-11-11`, from the themes branch (978e370) with this review's second pass merged in.
The owner asked for a more elegant, monochrome and clean UI with fine detail that stays clear in small spaces.
`public/css/app.css` is now one token system (it had grown as layers of overrides) and the monochrome look is the
default theme's values: the Appearance themes (light, dark, contrast, newsprint) set the same grey ramp, and every
other token derives from it. Module pages use the type tokens instead of their own pixel sizes. Information
architecture, features, ids and test selectors are unchanged, and the reading-list decisions stay (#121212 dark page,
`theme-color` from the page colour, `content-visibility` on the feed, story container queries, 68ch journal
paragraphs, 16px inputs, the WCAG 1.4.12 check).

**Tokens** (`:root` in `public/css/app.css`; dark under `prefers-color-scheme`; each `:root[data-theme]` block sets the ramp):

| Group | Tokens |
| --- | --- |
| Grey ramp | `--paper` #fff, `--wash` #f6f6f6, `--tint` #ececec, `--hairline` #e2e2e2, `--rule` #c2c2c2, `--mute` #616161, `--ink-2` #3a3a3a, `--ink` #141414. Dark: #121212, #1a1a1a, #262626, #2e2e2e, #4a4a4a, #a2a2a2, #cfcfcf, #ededed |
| Meaning colour | `--danger` #b42318 (dark #ff8b80) and `--danger-wash`, for errors, failing sources and destructive buttons only |
| Type, six sizes | `--fs-xs` 12, `--fs-sm` 13, `--fs-base` 16, `--fs-md` 18, `--fs-lg` 22, `--fs-xl` 30 (24 and 20 for the two largest under 640px; Large text moves every step up). Newsreader for headings and story titles, Plex Sans for the interface, Plex Mono for code |
| Space | `--sp-1` to `--sp-7`: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 |
| Lines, corners | `--hair` 1px (0.5px on 2x screens); radii `--r-mark` 4 (tags, badges), `--r-ctl` 6 (controls), `--r-surface` 10 (panels, cards), pills for topic filters |
| Controls | `--ctl-h` 32px drawn; 44px touch areas on phones through invisible hit zones |

The older names (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--fg`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--accent` and the rest) are aliases of the ramp,
so module styles follow every theme. `scripts/unslop.js` now also holds the ramp pairs (ink and mute on paper, wash
and tint; danger on paper and its wash) to 4.5:1 in every theme.

**What changed**

- Paper-white page; panels are hairline outlines, not grey-on-grey boxes; the List layout is a ruled list, Cards and Magazine keep their cards.
- Top bar: translucent, the current page marked by a thin ink rule on the bar's edge; menus and the page directory are floating sheets.
- States are tone, not hue: the sort control is a washed track with the chosen segment raised; tabs carry an ink rule; focus is a 2px ink ring.
- Significance: a tabular figure over a hairline gauge filled to the score (`--sig`), so "18/100" reads at a glance without a box.
- Story meta stays on one line: the source kind joins the source name, which truncates with the full text in its title; "+ 2 more sources" shows as "+ 2" on phones (the words stay for screen readers). Open item 9.
- Feed on a phone: heading and "Submit a link" share a line, topic pills draw at 32px with 44px tap zones, and the count, "Explain sort" and "Browse pages" sit on one quiet row. The first story starts at 396px instead of 470px (390×844, signed in). Open item 12.
- Story page: "Back to results" moved into the breadcrumb row; the score shows once (the detail card drops its badge; "Significance and evidence" shows it as a large figure); "Manage this record" sits in its own Administration row. Open item 13.
- All-caps labels in Arena, Project, Code map and Lab became sentence case; the explainer film's teal pulse is white.
- Links in running text and in lists get inline padding for a taller target without breaking the line (a Sources link no longer drops below its bullet on phones).
- A closed page directory is `display: none` in every engine (the capture had been counting its hidden links).

**Not changed, and why**

- Project and Arena stay full width: they are multi-pane workspaces, and the reading column would squeeze them. Reading pages use the column, workspaces use the window (open item 7).
- Chart and diagram text inside SVGs (Knowledge map, Lab and Arena charts, the concept map) keeps its own sizes, laid out against fixed geometry.
- Left to a person, as the reading list says: curly quotes across the copy, a colour accent or new palette, a reading-progress bar and page transitions.
