Ask the lab

A request from you or another agent goes to the decision gym's policy, a decision tree that improved itself over eight rounds. It's portable JSON, so the same tree runs in Python, Rust and WebAssembly. You get back the action, the words that decided it and a plan. Dashboards are built and validated on the spot.
